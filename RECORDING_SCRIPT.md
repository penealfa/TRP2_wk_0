# Recording script: የኮድ ቅርስ — The Heritage of Code

Language: Amharic. Narrator: Peneal (own voice, consent: ETHICS.md#peneal); the wolf is Gemini TTS

How to record: a quiet room with soft furnishings, phone 20-30 cm from your mouth, airplane mode on. Leave one second of silence before and after each line. Record each line as its own file and save it in `recordings/` under the name shown. Do two or three takes of the lines that matter most and keep the best one. Re-recording a line later only rebuilds that shot.

## s01  →  `recordings/s01.wav` (or .m4a)

> አሁን ሁሉም ሰው ፕሮግራመር ነው። ምኞቱን ይጽፋል፣ ማሽኑ ኮዱን ይጽፍለታል።

- Meaning: Everyone is a programmer now. They type a wish, and the machine writes the code.
- Shot on screen: Peneal sits at a small wooden desk at night in a modest Addis Ababa room, a laptop glowing blue in front of him, a cup of buna beside it, a window behind him with distant city lights
- Feeling: Open on Peneal alone at night so the viewer meets the person and his doubt before anything magical happens.
- Aim for about 4s (shot holds 7s)
